Which resistor, and where

Resistors

Every Mega pin can supply 20 mA at most. A resistor keeps LEDs and displays under that, and holds inputs at a steady level.

Quick rules

What you're connectingResistorWhere
An LED (any colour)220ΩIn series with the LED, on either leg
RGB LED3 × 220ΩOne on each colour leg
7-segment display220Ω eachOn the segment pins, or the digit pins (see the display's page)
Button or switch inputNoneUse INPUT_PULLUP, or a 10kΩ pull-down to GND
Thermistor or photocell10kΩ or 1kΩFrom the analog pin to GND, forming a divider
Transistor (PN2222, S8050)1kΩBetween the Mega pin and the base
Sensor modulesUsually noneThey have their own resistors on board
